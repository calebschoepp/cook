# Quiche

#quiche #eggs #bacon #french #brunch #pastry

> A classic Quiche Lorraine with a buttery pâte brisée crust, silky egg custard,
> bacon, leeks, and Gruyere.

## Ingredients

- *205 g* unsalted butter
- *250 g* bread flour
- *3 g* kosher salt
- *4 tbsp* ice water
- *11* eggs
- *260 g* whole milk
- *260 g* heavy cream
- *15 g* kosher salt
- *6 slices* bacon (thick-cut, chopped)
- *1* onion
- *2* leeks
- *150 g* gruyere cheese (cubed or shredded)
- *2 sprigs* fresh thyme (leaves picked)
- *1 g* nutmeg
- black pepper

## Cookware

- pie dish
- oven
- immersion blender

## Steps

1. Make the short-crust dough (pâte brisée). Chill the dry ingredients, bowl,
and paddle in the freezer for (20) minutes, and keep the ice water cold in the
fridge. Mix unsalted butter and bread flour on low speed until it looks like
coarse sand with a few medium clumps, about (5)-10 minutes. Add kosher salt
along the way.

2. Add the ice water and mix until a smooth ball forms. Shape into a puck on a
floured surface, wrap in plastic, and refrigerate at least (2) hours (overnight
is better).

3. Roll the dough straight from the fridge to about 1/8 inch thick. Lay it over
your pie dish and gently press it into the mold, then trim the excess. Chill
again for (30) minutes.

4. Blind bake the crust in a oven at 400°F for (25)-30 minutes, then remove the
foil and bake another (5)-10 minutes until golden.

5. Make the egg base. Blend eggs, whole milk, heavy cream, and kosher salt with
an immersion blender, avoiding incorporating too much air.

6. Cook the bacon until crisp-tender, then drain. Thinly slice the onion and
leeks.

7. Layer the bacon, onion, and leeks into the pre-baked crust, then add the
gruyere cheese. Sprinkle over the fresh thyme, a few microplane passes of
nutmeg, and black pepper to taste. Pour the egg base over the top.

8. Bake at 350°F for (40)-60 minutes until just set and barely jiggly in the
middle.

9. Rest the quiche at least (25) minutes before slicing. Remove it from the mold
and let it cool another (10) minutes, then slice into portions and serve.
