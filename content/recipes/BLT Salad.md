# BLT Salad

#salad #kale #bacon #healthy

> A hearty shredded kale salad with crispy bacon and juicy cherry tomatoes,
> tossed in a creamy homemade Greek yogurt ranch dressing.

## Ingredients

- *1 large head* kale (stems removed and shredded)
- *1 tbsp* olive oil
- *1/4 tsp* salt
- *1/4 tsp* pepper
- *4 slices* thick-cut bacon (chopped)
- *1/3 c* greek yogurt
- *2 tbsp* mayonnaise
- *1 tsp* parsley (freshly chopped)
- *1 tsp* dill (freshly chopped)
- *1 clove* garlic (minced)
- *1/2 tsp* worcestershire sauce
- *1/4 tsp* white vinegar
- *1/4 tsp* smoked paprika
- *1/8 tsp* onion powder
- *1/8 tsp* salt
- *1/8 tsp* pepper
- *3 tbsp* olive oil
- *1 c* cherry tomatoes (halved)

## Cookware

- large bowl
- skillet
- food processor

## Steps

1. Place kale in a large bowl, drizzle with olive oil, and add salt and pepper.
Massage the oil in by hand for a few minutes, then set aside.

2. Heat a skillet over medium-low, add the thick-cut bacon, and cook until
totally crispy, stirring occasionally and the fat is rendered, about 10 minutes.

3. To make the dressing, blend greek yogurt, mayonnaise, parsley, dill, garlic,
worcestershire sauce, white vinegar, smoked paprika, onion powder, salt, pepper,
and olive oil in a food processor until combined.

4. Add the cherry tomatoes and crispy bacon to the massaged kale, drizzle with
the dressing, and toss to combine. Top with optional sliced avocado, crumbled
feta or blue cheese, and sweet corn if desired.
