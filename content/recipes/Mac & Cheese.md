# Mac & Cheese

#gluten-free #pasta #cheese #comfort-food #baked

> A creamy, three-cheese gluten-free mac and cheese baked with a buttery cracker
> topping for the ultimate comfort food.

## Ingredients

- *1 lb* gluten-free elbow macaroni
- *1 tbsp* olive oil
- *4 tbsp* unsalted butter
- *4 tbsp* gluten-free all-purpose flour
- *5 c* milk
- *1 tsp* onion powder
- *1 tsp* garlic powder
- *1 tsp* paprika
- *1 tsp* mustard powder
- *1/4 tsp* red pepper flakes
- *1/4 tsp* salt
- *1/2 c* sour cream
- salt
- pepper
- *2 c* sharp cheddar cheese (grated)
- *1 c* white Colby cheese (grated)
- *1 c* Parmesan cheese (grated)
- *1 1/2 c* gluten-free buttery crackers (crushed)
- *6 tbsp* melted unsalted butter
- *1 c* white Colby cheese (grated)
- *1/2 c* cheddar cheese (grated)
- *1/2 c* Parmesan cheese (grated)

## Cookware

- 9x13 baking dish
- large pot
- large bowl
- large saucepan

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and lightly grease a 9x13 baking dish and set
aside.

2. In a large pot, cook gluten-free elbow macaroni to al dente, then drain and
rinse under hot water. Toss with olive oil in a large bowl and set aside.

3. In a large saucepan over medium heat, melt the unsalted butter. Add the
gluten-free all-purpose flour and whisk until fully combined.

4. Gradually add milk while whisking until smooth.

5. Add the onion powder, garlic powder, paprika, mustard powder, red pepper
flakes, and salt.

6. Bring to a boil over medium heat and simmer for 2 minutes.

7. Stir in the sour cream and adjust salt and pepper to taste.

8. Reduce the heat to low and add the sharp cheddar cheese, white Colby cheese,
and Parmesan cheese, stirring until melted.

9. Combine the sauce with the cooked pasta, then transfer the mixture to the
prepared baking dish.

10. For the topping, mix the gluten-free buttery crackers with melted unsalted
butter.

11. Sprinkle the reserved white Colby cheese, cheddar cheese, and Parmesan
cheese evenly over the top, then add the buttery cracker topping.

12. Bake uncovered until golden brown and bubbly, 30 minutes.

13. Rest for 15 minutes before serving.
