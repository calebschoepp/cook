# Scalloped Potatoes and Ham

#potatoes #ham #casserole #comfort food #gluten free

> Layers of thinly sliced Yukon Gold potatoes baked in a creamy cheddar sauce
> until bubbly and golden, served alongside a baked ham.

## Ingredients

- *4 tbsp* unsalted butter
- *2 c* whole milk
- *4 tbsp* gluten-free all-purpose flour
- *1/2 tbsp* salt
- *1/4 tsp* black pepper
- *1 tbsp* onion powder
- *1 tbsp* garlic powder
- *1 tsp* dried thyme
- *1 c* chicken broth
- *4 lbs* Yukon Gold potatoes (sliced 1/4-inch thick)
- *1/2 c* shredded Parmesan cheese
- *1 c* shredded cheddar cheese
- *1 c* shredded cheddar cheese

## Cookware

- oven
- 9x13 baking dish
- medium saucepan

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 400F. Grease a 9x13 baking dish with cooking spray.

2. Melt unsalted butter in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Add whole
milk and whisk to combine.

3. Whisk in gluten-free all-purpose flour, then stir in salt, black pepper,
onion powder, garlic powder, and dried thyme.

4. Add chicken broth and whisk. Cook for (saucepan)2 minutes until it just
begins to simmer around the edges and thickens, then remove from heat.

5. Spread half of the Yukon Gold potatoes evenly in the greased baking dish. Top
with half the cream sauce.

6. Sprinkle shredded Parmesan cheese and shredded cheddar cheese over the layer.

7. Add the remaining potatoes, the remaining sauce, and the remaining shredded
cheddar cheese.

8. Cover with foil and bake for (oven)30 minutes. Remove the foil and bake
uncovered for another (oven)30 minutes, until the potatoes are cooked through
and the top is golden.

9. Let cool a few minutes before serving warm.

> **Note:** Note: Cook a ham alongside the potatoes and serve the two together.
