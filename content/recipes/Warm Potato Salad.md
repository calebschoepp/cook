# Warm Potato Salad

#potato #bacon #warm salad #roasted

> Earl's-inspired warm salad of roasted baby potatoes and corn tossed with
> crispy bacon and a creamy Parmesan dressing.

## Ingredients

- *2/3 c* mayonnaise
- *1/3 c* sour cream
- *4 tbsp* parmesan cheese (finely grated)
- *1 tbsp* lemon juice
- *1 tbsp* chives (finely chopped)
- salt
- pepper
- *4 slices* bacon
- *2 lbs* baby potatoes
- *1-2 cloves* garlic (minced)
- *3 tbsp* olive oil
- *1 c* corn kernels (drained if frozen)
- *1 tbsp* olive oil
- chives

## Cookware

- oven
- skillet
- parchment-lined sheet pan
- large bowl

## Steps

1. In a small bowl, combine mayonnaise, sour cream, parmesan cheese, lemon
juice, chives and season lightly with salt and pepper to make the dressing.
Season lightly since the bacon and potatoes add saltiness; you can adjust later.

2. Preheat the oven to 425°F.

3. In a skillet, fry bacon over medium heat until crispy, then drain, cool, and
chop into ½-inch pieces.

4. Halve baby potatoes, toss with garlic and olive oil, spread on a parchment-
lined sheet pan, and bake for 20 minutes.

5. Toss corn kernels with the remaining olive oil, add to the potatoes, and bake
another 25 minutes.

6. Transfer the potatoes and corn to a large bowl, toss with the dressing and
bacon, adjust seasoning, garnish with chives for garnish, and serve.
