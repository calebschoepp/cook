# Korean Cheesy Corn

#korean #corn #cheese #side

> A creamy, gooey Korean-style side dish of sweet corn baked with mayonnaise and
> mozzarella until bubbly and golden.

## Ingredients

- *2 ears* sweet corn kernels (kernels removed from cob)
- *1/2 c* mayonnaise
- *4 oz* shredded mozzarella
- *1/2 tbsp* sugar
- *2* green onions (thinly sliced)
- salt
- pepper

## Cookware

- oven
- mixing bowl
- baking dish

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.

2. In a mixing bowl, combine sweet corn kernels, mayonnaise, shredded
mozzarella, sugar, green onions, salt and pepper, and stir together.

3. Pour the mixture into a lightly greased baking dish.

4. Bake for 10 minutes, or until the mixture melts and bubbles.

5. Transfer the dish to the broiler and broil for 2 minutes, or until the top
browns.

6. Serve immediately.
