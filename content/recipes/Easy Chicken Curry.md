# Easy Chicken Curry

#chicken #curry #coconut #weeknight #one-pan

> A comforting one-pan chicken curry with tender chicken simmered in a spiced
> tomato and coconut sauce, finished with baby spinach.

## Ingredients

- *2 tbsp* vegetable oil
- *3* chicken breasts (approx 500g, chopped into bite-size chunks)
- *1 large* onion (peeled and finely chopped)
- *2 cloves* garlic (minced)
- *2 tsp* minced ginger
- *2 tbsp* mild curry powder
- *1 tbsp* ground coriander
- *1/2 tbsp* ground cumin
- *1 tsp* paprika
- *1/2 tsp* ground cinnamon
- *1/2 tsp* salt
- *1/2 tsp* black pepper
- *2 tbsp* tomato paste
- *400 g* finely chopped tomatoes (1 tin)
- *240 ml* chicken stock
- *400 ml* full fat coconut milk (1 tin)
- *1 tbsp* cornflour (mixed with 2 tbsp cold water to make a slurry)
- *60 g* baby spinach (2 packed cups)
- boiled rice
- fresh coriander
- chilli flakes

## Cookware

- large pan

## Steps

1. Heat vegetable oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Add chicken breasts
and cook for 5-6 minutes until browned all over. Remove the chicken to a plate
and set aside.

2. Lower the heat to medium and add onion. Cook for 5 minutes until softened,
then stir in garlic and minced ginger and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.

3. Stir in mild curry powder, ground coriander, ground cumin, paprika, ground
cinnamon, salt, and black pepper. Cook for 1 minute to toast the spices.

4. Add tomato paste and stir to combine, then pour in finely chopped tomatoes,
chicken stock, and full fat coconut milk. Stir well.

5. Return the chicken to the pan, bring to a simmer, and cook for 15 minutes,
stirring occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has
thickened.

6. For a thicker sauce, stir in cornflour and simmer for another 2 minutes.

7. Stir in baby spinach and cook until just wilted, 1-2 minutes.

8. Serve over boiled rice and garnish with fresh coriander and chilli flakes.
