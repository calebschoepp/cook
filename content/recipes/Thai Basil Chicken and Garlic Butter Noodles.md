# Thai Basil Chicken and Garlic Butter Noodles

#chicken #noodles #thai #sheet-pan #weeknight

> Sheet-pan curry-spiced chicken and asparagus served over rice noodles tossed
> in a garlicky coconut butter sauce with fresh Thai basil.

## Ingredients

- *1 lb* chicken breasts (thinly sliced)
- *1* shallot (sliced)
- *2 tbsp* yellow curry powder
- *1-2 tsp* black pepper
- chili flakes
- *2 tbsp* ghee
- *2 tbsp* fish sauce
- *2 tsp* honey
- *1 bunch* asparagus (chopped)
- *1 tbsp* ghee
- salt
- pepper
- *1/2 c* Thai basil
- *3 tbsp* salted butter
- *4 cloves* garlic (chopped)
- chili flakes
- *1 c* coconut milk
- salt
- *8 oz* rice noodles
- basil
- green onions (sliced)
- limes

## Cookware

- baking sheet
- skillet

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.

2. On a baking sheet, mix the chicken breasts with the shallot, yellow curry
powder, black pepper, and chili flakes. Toss to coat, then add ghee, fish sauce,
and honey, and toss again. Arrange the chicken in an even layer. Arrange the
asparagus to the side of the chicken and toss with ghee. Season with salt and
pepper.

3. Bake for 15-20 minutes, until the chicken is cooked. Then toss with the Thai
basil.

4. Meanwhile, make the noodles. Melt the salted butter in a skillet set over
medium heat. Add the garlic and a pinch of chili flakes. Cook until the garlic
begins to caramelize and turn light golden brown, 1-2 minutes. Stir in the
coconut milk and cook for 3-5 minutes, until warmed through. Season with salt.
Remove from the heat and toss the rice noodles with the sauce.

5. To serve, divide the noodles among bowls and top with chicken and asparagus.
Serve with additional basil, green onions, and limes. Enjoy!
