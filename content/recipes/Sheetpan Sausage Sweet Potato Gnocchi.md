# Sheetpan Sausage Sweet Potato Gnocchi

> Cozy sheetpan dinner of sausage, sweet potato, red onion, and gnocchi roasted until browned, finished with goat cheese and hot honey.

## Ingredients

- *1 lb* sausage (spicy Italian is great, or whatever your family likes)
- *1* red onion (chopped)
- *1 pack* gnocchi
- *1* sweet potato (cubed)
- *2 tbsp* olive oil
- *1 tsp* garlic salt
- *1 tsp* dried thyme (or rosemary or any dried herb)
- goat cheese (crumbled)
- hot honey (or regular honey)

## Cookware

- oven
- sheet pan

## Steps

1. Preheat the oven to 425F.

2. On a sheet pan, toss sausage, red onion, gnocchi, and sweet potato with olive oil, garlic salt, and dried thyme. Spread into an even layer.

3. Bake for 25-35 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until browned and the sweet potato is tender.

4. Finish with goat cheese and a drizzle of hot honey.
