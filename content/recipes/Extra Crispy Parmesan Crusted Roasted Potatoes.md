# Extra Crispy Parmesan Crusted Roasted Potatoes

#potatoes #roasted #parmesan #crispy #side dish

> Parboiled russet potatoes tossed with butter and Parmesan into a starchy
> slurry, then roasted until they develop a shatteringly crisp cheese crust over
> a fluffy interior.

## Ingredients

- *3 lbs* russet potatoes (peeled and cut into 1 1/2- to 2-inch chunks)
- *1/2 tsp* baking soda
- *2 tbsp* kosher salt
- *4* bay leaves
- *1 tbsp* black peppercorns
- *3 cloves* garlic (roughly crushed)
- *4 tbsp* unsalted butter (melted)
- *2 oz* grated parmesan cheese (about 1 cup)
- salt

## Cookware

- oven
- sheet pan
- pot
- sheet pan

## Steps

1. Adjust the oven rack to the center position and heat the oven to 425F (or
400F with convection). Line a sheet pan with parchment paper.

2. Combine russet potatoes, baking soda, kosher salt, bay leaves, black
peppercorns and garlic in a large pot. Cover with cold water by about an inch
and bring to a boil over high heat.

3. Reduce to a simmer and cook until the potatoes are tender all the way to the
center, about (stovetop)10 minutes. Drain well, discarding the bay leaves and
peppercorns, and return the potatoes to the pot.

4. Add unsalted butter and grated parmesan cheese to the hot potatoes. Toss and
fold with a rubber spatula until the Parmesan, butter and starch form a slurry
over the surface of the potatoes, about 30 seconds.

5. Transfer the potatoes to the prepared sheet pan and spread them out so
they are mostly separated from one another. Roast until pale golden brown and
sizzling on the bottom, about (oven)20 minutes.

6. Flip the potatoes using a thin metal spatula and continue roasting until
crisp and deep gold on most sides, about (oven)15 minutes longer. Watch
carefully toward the end and do not let them brown beyond a deep gold, or the
Parmesan will turn bitter.

7. Remove from the oven and let cool for (rest)5 minutes on the pan. Transfer to
a serving platter, season with salt to taste and serve.
